## Hook (0:00–0:12)
WORDS:
If your best work only happens when you feel inspired, you do not have a work problem. You have a start-cue problem, and that is cheaper to fix.

VISUAL:

## Payoff promise
WORDS:
In the next few minutes you will build a default that starts the task before motivation arrives.

## Beat 1 — Why motivation is a bad scheduler
WORDS:
Motivation is a weather report. Useful to notice. Useless as a calendar. If the plan depends on a feeling, the plan is optional.

VISUAL:
*Calendar vs cloud icon contrast*

## Beat 2 — The two-step default
WORDS:
Write one start cue you already meet every day. Then attach a two-minute first action that is so small you cannot negotiate it.

VISUAL:
*Cue -> two-minute action diagram*

## Beat 3 — Decide it once
WORDS:
Do not choose the first action in the morning. Choose it the night before. Decision fatigue is where the stall hides.

VISUAL:
*Night note on desk*

## Beat 4 — Measure starts, not moods
WORDS:
Track only whether the two-minute action happened. Ignore how you felt. Feeling follows the start more often than the reverse.

VISUAL:
*Simple checkbox row*

## CTA
WORDS:
Pick tomorrow’s cue and two-minute action before you close this video. That is the whole assignment.
