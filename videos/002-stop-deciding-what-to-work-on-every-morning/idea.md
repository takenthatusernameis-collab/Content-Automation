# Idea

- topic: Stop deciding what to work on every morning
- viewer: People who already plan their week, then stall when it is time to start.
- problem: they reopen the whole work list every morning instead of starting
- mechanism (the system, not the pep talk): Install a written next-action default so the morning does not reopen the whole list.
- why now: The week is already planned, but the morning still reopens the whole list.
